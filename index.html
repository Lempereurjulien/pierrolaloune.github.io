About Me

I am a PhD student and geographer specializing in community ecology and functional diversity. My research focuses on how human activities reshape biodiversity patterns and the functional diversity of vertebrates at global and macroecological scales. More broadly, I work on trait-based ecology, macroecology, conservation biology, and the impacts of anthropogenic pressures on ecosystems.

I am also actively engaged in scientific outreach through associations such as Pint of Science and Les étoiles brillent pour tous, contributing to the dissemination and accessibility of scientific knowledge.

Alongside my research, I teach undergraduate courses at the University of Toulouse, including Zoology, Aquatic Ecology, and Chemical Ecology.

Research Interests

  • Functional ecology and functional diversity
  • Macroecology and biogeography
  • Conservation biology
  • Human impacts on biodiversity
  • Trait-based approaches
