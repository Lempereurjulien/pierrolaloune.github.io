About

profile

I am a PhD student in macroecology and biogeography at the CRBE, University of Toulouse, supervised by Sébastien Brosse and Aurèle Toussaint.

I’m interested in how human uses of vertebrates (fisheries, trade, recreation, hunt, etc.) translate into non-random patterns in functional space, and why that matters for conservation.

Research

what I do

My thesis examines how humans selectively exploit vertebrate species and how this selection reshapes functional diversity at global scales.

  • Functional traits influencing human use (fisheries, hunting, trade, recreation).
  • Links between exploitation, extinction risk, and invasivness.
  • Non random pattern in functional trait-space.
