About Me

I'm a PhD student, geographer specialized in community ecology and functional diversity. I work on the global human impact on functional diversity among vertebrates. My research focuses on human impact on biodiversity, macroecology, conservation of biodiversity, and functional traits.

I am actively involved in science vulgarisation through associations such as Pint of Science and Les étoiles brillent pour tous.

I also teach undergraduate students at the University of Toulouse: Zoology, Aquatic Ecology, Chemical Ecology.
